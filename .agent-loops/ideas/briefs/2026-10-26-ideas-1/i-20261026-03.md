### Idea 3 (i-20261026-03) · Find the account that can still run Opus (or Sonnet) this week, and switch to it in one click

**Their problem today.** Claude stops you at the 5-hour session, the all-models weekly, or, on some plans, a weekly cap for one model. RevvyTach judges room from the first two only. So its account lists call an account free when its Opus cap is full, and auto-switch can move your terminal onto it. Per-model figures sit inside each popover, so you open accounts one by one.

**Their reality after.** You pick the model you need (default: all models). Every account row shows the room left for it and names the limit that binds. Rows sort by that room, Make Active on each. Auto-switch lands only where that model runs. A "Compare accounts" window shows every limit. A dash means no reading, never 0%.

**Where you'd see it:** a "Room for" picker atop the health strip's account list and the "+N" list; beside auto-switch in Settings; "Compare accounts" at both lists' foot.

**For example** (illustrative numbers)**.** Opus fills on "Work Max". Pick Opus: "Personal Max" 30% left (Opus cap); "Side Max" 8% (all-models weekly). Make Active on "Personal Max".

**As a** developer with five to ten accounts, **when** one model's limit stops me, **I want** accounts ranked by room left for that model **so that** my terminal lands where it can run.

**Target user:** developer with several Claude/Codex accounts · **Strategy:** "I can see at a glance which of my accounts still has headroom and put my terminal on it in one click"

**Already tracked:** posted most-room ranking idea (not model-aware). **What this adds:** model-aware room in rows, ranking and auto-switch.

**Why it matters.** Job 1, finding headroom, plus job 4's auto-switch. Today "room" is wrong whenever a model cap binds.

**Cost.** Effort L (first slice M). **Biggest risk.** Limit stacking is undocumented upstream.
**An agent could build this unattended:** yes (first slice).
**Score** 20 of 25 = job value 4 × strategic fit 5. Demand none. Risk 3, effort L.
