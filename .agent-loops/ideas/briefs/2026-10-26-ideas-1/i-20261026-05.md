### Idea 5 (i-20261026-05) · Keep work, personal and client accounts from mixing

**Their problem today.** You hold a Max, a spare Pro and a client's Team seat. On every switch, MCP sync (on by default) copies every account's MCP servers into every other: the client's servers and saved keys land in your accounts, yours in theirs. All accounts share one skills folder. Automatic switching can land your side project on it. Your only defences: sync off everywhere, or no automation.

**Their reality after.** Each account gets a label: Work, Personal, or a client's name. Only same-label accounts share MCP servers and skills. Automatic switching and the Next Profile hotkey stay inside your label and ask before leaving it; Make Active by hand goes anywhere. It lists servers that already crossed, for removal.

**Where you'd see it:** Settings → Manage Profiles, a label picker per account; the MCP sync card in Settings → CLI Account; a tag in the popover's Accounts list.

**For example** (illustrative numbers)**.** Acme's seat has an "acme-jira" server with a key. Today one switch copies it into all three accounts; after, it stays with Acme. Max runs out at 3:10 pm with Pro at 60% and Acme at 12%: you land on Pro.

**As a** developer with a client seat, **when** I switch accounts, **I want** a line between work, personal and client accounts **so that** their servers, keys and work never mix.

**Target user:** Team/Enterprise member · **Strategy:** "many accounts, two providers, one active slot each, the terminal following via tmux and rc pointers"

**Already tracked:** nothing open; related: i-20261025-02 (conversation sharing, posted), i-20261025-14 (per-server MCP control, held). **What this adds:** one label per account that every crossing honours, absorbing i-20261025-02's "work seats apart" rule.

**Why it matters.** Keeps accounts consistent (job 6) without breaking the org-versus-personal split; makes automatic switching (job 4) safe.

**Cost.** Effort L. **Biggest risk.** It changes a merge that writes into other accounts' settings.
**An agent could build this unattended:** no.
**Score** 12 of 25 = job value 4 × strategic fit 3. Demand none. Risk 3, effort L.
