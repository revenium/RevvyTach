### Idea 6 (i-20261026-06) · When Opus runs out on one account, tell me which account still has it

**Their problem today.** Claude caps some models separately inside the weekly allowance. Opus can run out mid-week while the all-models weekly still has room. RevvyTach stays silent. Its room checks and auto-switch count only session and weekly, so an Opus-spent account looks free. You open each account to find Opus elsewhere.

**Their reality after.** When a model's cap fills, one notification names the account with the most room for that model, counting session, all-models weekly and that model's cap. Clicking it opens that account, with Make Active one click away. It suggests another model on the same account only if its all-models weekly has room.

**Where you'd see it:** a macOS notification, plus a popover notice under the per-model bars.

**For example** (illustrative numbers)**.** Wednesday, "Work Max": Opus 100%, all-models 64%. Notification: "Opus is spent on Work Max until Monday. Personal Max has 70% Opus left. Sonnet still works here." Two clicks, back on Opus.

**As a** developer with several Claude Max or Team accounts, **when** one model's cap fills on my active account, **I want** to be told which account still has it **so that** I keep the model I need.

**Target user:** developer with several Claude accounts; Team/Enterprise member · **Strategy:** "When I hit a Claude or Codex limit, I can see at a glance which of my accounts still has headroom and put my terminal on it in one click"

**Already tracked:** nothing open; near yesterday's most-room ranking and weekly-wall warning, and today's per-model comparison. **What this adds:** the alert at a model's wall, which the weekly-wall warning never fires on.

**Why it matters.** It serves job 1 (find headroom at the limiter) and job 4 (warned before redline) for per-model limits.
