### Idea 1 (i-20261025-01) · Know which account has the most room, who is back first, and how long yours will last

**Their problem today.** A developer with six Claude accounts hits "limit reached" mid-task and does clock arithmetic across six rows of percentages and reset times. Automatic switching takes the next account in list order, even one with 3% left. When all are capped, "session reset" alerts trickle in, some for accounts still weekly-blocked; none says where to go.

**Their reality after.** One ranking answers: "work-2 has the most room · work-1 back in 14 min". When nobody has room: "work-3 back first, at 3:10 pm", then one "work-3 has room again" alert instead of per-account reset alerts. The same ranking can steer automatic switching and the next-account shortcut. Later, the active account says "lasts about 40 more minutes at your pace". Too little data shows nothing.

**Where you'd see it:** the popover, atop its Accounts list; the combined menu bar item's account list; a macOS notification.

**For example** (illustrative numbers)**.** At 3:00 pm all six are capped; work-5 until Thursday. The popover reads "work-3 back first, at 3:10 pm". At 3:10 one alert arrives; one Make Active click resumes work.

**As a** developer with several Claude/Codex accounts, **when** I hit a limit, **I want** accounts ranked by usable room and return time **so that** I choose wait or switch in seconds.

**Target user:** developer with several Claude/Codex accounts · **Strategy:** "I can see at a glance which of my accounts still has headroom and put my terminal on it in one click".

**Already tracked:** nothing open (the auto-switch retry bug is separate). **What this adds:** a headroom ranking and first-back alert, later a time-left forecast.

**Why it matters.** It serves the first job, headroom at the limiter: the app has every number but leaves ranking to a blocked user.

**Cost.** Effort M. **Biggest risk.** A confident wrong time, from pace compared across plans.
**An agent could build this unattended:** yes.
**Score** 20 of 25 = job value 4 × strategic fit 5. Demand none. Risk 4, effort M.
