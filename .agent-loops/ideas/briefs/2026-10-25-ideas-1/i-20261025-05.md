### Idea 5 (i-20261025-05) · Every switch lands where unused weekly room is about to expire

**Their problem today.** A developer with four Claude accounts, each resetting weekly on a different day, hits a limit. Auto-switch and the Next Profile shortcut take the next account in list order that isn't full, even one at 99%. So work-2, resetting tonight two-thirds unused, is skipped and that paid week lapses.

**Their reality after.** Every switch keeps only accounts with real room in both limits, then takes the one whose week resets soonest, so expiring room is used first. If none clears the bar, the one with most room. The Accounts list marks it "Use first". No reminders, no forecasts. Later, a weekly outlook line, shown only when it holds even if your pace is a third off, and a Week view of each account's reset and room left.

**Where you'd see it:** the popover's Accounts list; where auto-switch and Next Profile land; later, a Week window beside Manage Profiles.

**For example** (illustrative numbers)**.** Tuesday 10:00, work-1 hits its limit. Next in the list is work-3 (12% used, resets Sunday); work-2 is 38% used, resets at 16:00. Auto-switch picks work-2; its 62% is used, not lost.

**As a** developer with several Claude/Codex accounts, **when** I hit a limit and switch, **I want** to land on the account whose unused week expires soonest **so that** I keep working and no paid week goes to waste.

**Target user:** developer with several Claude/Codex accounts · **Strategy:** "see at a glance which of my accounts still has headroom and put my terminal on it".

**Already tracked:** nothing open. **What this adds:** one rule for every switch, and a mark showing it.

**Why it matters.** At the limiter (JTBD-1, find headroom) the app picks by list position. The expiring week is capacity already paid for.

**Cost.** Effort M (first slice S). **Biggest risk.** An expiring but nearly spent week means stopping again sooner.
**An agent could build this unattended:** no.
**Score** 15 of 25 = job value 3 × strategic fit 5. Demand none. Risk 2, effort M.

**Risk in full.**

- Behaviour change: auto-switch users see a different destination than before; changelog it.
- Localization: one string, nine translations.
- Codex: ProfileResetOrder is Claude-only; Codex keeps list order until its windows are mapped.
- Later slices under the honest-number rule (failure mode 1): the Week view shows only facts (reset time, room left) plus the already shipped pace marker. The outlook line ("your accounts last past Friday's reset" / "run out before it") is shown only when the conclusion is unchanged with every measured burn rate scaled by 0.7 and 1.3, with each rate measured only while that account was active and any unmeasured account that could flip the answer turning the line off. Otherwise it shows nothing.

**Alternatives.**
- Do nothing: the `+N` list already sorts by reset, but the switch ignores it.
- Idea 3's "most room now" for every switch: simpler, but always drains the freshest week and lets expiring ones lapse; kept here as the fallback and as the room bar.
